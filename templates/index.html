    How it works

    1. Enter your budget

      Choose a planner and fill in a short form. Quick amounts save typing.

    2. Get a checked plan

      Gemini proposes the items. The server rechecks every price and removes anything that would take you over budget.

    3. Shop and come back

      Open the store links for each item. Every plan is saved in your history.

    AI proposes. PocketSmart verifies.

    A visible spending limit

    Planned spend and remaining budget are calculated by the server instead of trusting the AI's arithmetic.

    A fallback when AI is unavailable

    If the AI service is unavailable, the application can return a clearly labelled standard estimate instead of failing the whole plan.

    Your plans stay yours

    Passwords are hashed, sessions expire after 30 minutes, and only you can open your saved plans.
