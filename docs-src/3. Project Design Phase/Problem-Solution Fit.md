# Problem-Solution Fit Canvas

{{HEADER:5 Marks}}

| Customer Segment | Customer Limitations | Existing Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Students, young professionals, families and first-time home planners who need to make a group of purchases within one fixed amount | Limited time, fixed spending limit, uncertainty about category-wise costs, many stores and service providers | Manual browsing, notes/calculators, spreadsheets, asking friends, single-store shopping |

| Problems / Pains | Root Cause | Current Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Users know the maximum amount but struggle to distribute it across several needs. Repeated comparison increases planning time. | Cost information and recommendations are fragmented; most shopping tools focus on individual items rather than the complete budget | Users open several tabs, make rough calculations, remove items after the total becomes too high and repeat the process |

| Triggers | Proposed Solution | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| Moving into a home, arranging an event, preparing for a wedding/festival or making a planned purchase | PocketSmart AI starts with the total budget, creates a category-wise plan using Gemini, verifies the total in the backend and provides search links for further shopping | Web browser, GitHub-hosted project, external retailer/service search pages |

| Before Using | After Using |
|---|---|
| Unclear allocation, too many choices, repeated calculations | A structured plan, visible category totals, remaining amount and saved history |

## Fit Statement

The proposed solution fits the problem because it focuses on the part that existing shopping sites do not solve together: **planning the whole spending limit before selecting individual items**.
