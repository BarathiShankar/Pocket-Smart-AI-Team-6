## Define Problem Statements

**Project idea:** PocketSmart AI is a budget-first planning assistant. Instead of asking the user to search for products first, it starts with the total amount the user is willing to spend and creates a practical allocation for the complete task. The plan is then checked by the application before it is shown.

| Problem Statement | I am | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS-1 | A person setting up a home on a fixed amount | Plan essential furniture and fittings without exhausting the budget early | I do not know how much to reserve for each requirement | Products are spread across several stores and prices vary widely | Uncertain about where to start |
| PS-2 | Someone arranging a family or social event | Divide one event budget among venue, food, decoration and entertainment | One expensive decision can affect the rest of the event | Event costs are usually considered separately instead of as one plan | Worried about last-minute compromises |
| PS-3 | A customer preparing for an occasion | Choose jewelry that suits the occasion and stays within a spending limit | Too many styles make comparison difficult | Online catalogues are large and rarely explain the trade-off between style and budget | Overloaded by choices |
| PS-4 | A student or young professional with limited planning time | Get a usable spending plan quickly | Manual comparison takes too long | There is no single place that combines allocation, recommendations and budget checking | Frustrated by repetitive searching |

## Core Problem

People often know **how much they can spend**, but not **how that amount should be distributed**. PocketSmart AI addresses this gap by turning a total budget and a few practical preferences into a structured plan that can be reviewed, adjusted and used for shopping.
