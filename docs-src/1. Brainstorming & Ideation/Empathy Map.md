# Empathy Map

## Primary Persona

**Persona: Ananya, 26, early-career professional who frequently plans purchases and small events with a fixed monthly or occasion-based limit.**

| SAYS | THINKS |
|---|---|
| “I know my maximum budget, but I don't know how to divide it.”<br>“I don't want to compare ten websites for every item.”<br>“Show me alternatives if the first plan is too expensive.” | “If I spend too much on one category, I will have to reduce something important later.”<br>“I want a recommendation, but I still want control over the final purchase.”<br>“The total should be easy to verify.” |

| **PERSONA: ANANYA** | |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Complete a home, event or occasion-related plan without crossing a self-imposed spending limit. |
| **Main Need** | A clear allocation, understandable recommendations and a record of the final plan. |

| DOES | FEELS |
|---|---|
| Opens multiple shopping and service websites.<br>Keeps rough totals in notes or a calculator.<br>Asks friends for suggestions when unsure.<br>Changes items when the running total becomes too high. | Uncertain at the beginning.<br>Overwhelmed when there are many choices.<br>Relieved when the allocation is clear.<br>More confident when the final amount is independently checked. |

## Design Insight

The user does not need the AI to make every purchase decision. They need the AI to reduce the amount of planning work while keeping the **budget, item list and final calculation visible**.
